# Bloomshift Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Bloomshift is a mobile puzzle game published by Görsen Games. This policy
explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information stored on your device

Bloomshift does not require an account. Game progress, settings, language,
earned in-game currency, purchase entitlements, and level results are stored
locally on your device. Removing the app may also remove this local data.

## Advertising

Bloomshift uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded and interstitial advertising.
Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and platform settings,
Google and its partners may process information such as:

- Device or advertising identifiers
- Advertising interactions and product interaction data
- Approximate region inferred from network information
- Crash reports, diagnostics, and performance data

Advertising availability and personalization may vary by region. Where
required, advertising is shown according to the consent and privacy choices
available on the device. For details about Google's data practices, see
[Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through
Google Play. Görsen Games does not receive or store your full payment-card
details. The app may retain purchase status and transaction identifiers needed
to deliver or restore purchased features and prevent duplicate rewards.

## Data sharing

Görsen Games does not sell personal information. Information may be processed
by Apple, Google, and their service providers only as needed to distribute the
app, process purchases, deliver advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain their
platform services.

## Children

Bloomshift is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly request
names, contact details, or account information from children. Advertising and
store services remain subject to the age and family policies of Apple and
Google.

## Your choices

You can control sound, music, and haptics in the app. Advertising and privacy
choices may also be available through your device settings, platform account,
or a consent prompt where required. Local game data can be removed by clearing
the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The
latest public version is available at
[gorsengames.com/privacy-policy](https://gorsengames.com/privacy-policy).
